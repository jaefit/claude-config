# Preserve list

Applies in both languages. Detection may mention these; rewriting leaves them byte-identical.

## Always
- Numbers, units, dates, percentages, bp, %p, currency amounts, ratios
- Proper nouns, org names, team names, product/screen names (TIS, IMDP, ECOS, KOFIA, 인포맥스, 금감원)
- Quoted text inside " " or 「 」, regulation article numbers (제3-46조), legal phrases quoted from a rule
- Code, inline code, commands, paths, URLs, YAML, table cell values, formulas
- Heading meaning (fix case or decoration, not the words)

## Domain terms (FICC / rates / FX / credit). English inside Korean text is NOT KO-B2 for these
IRS, CRS, OIS, SOFR, KOFR, CD, KTB, MSB, NDF, FX swap, basis, spread, carry, roll-down, roll, DV01, PV01, delta, gamma, vega, convexity, tenor, curve, steepener, flattener, butterfly, box, receive/pay, bid/offer, mark, MTM, P&L, PnL, VaR, notional, outright, forward, spot, swap point, cross, funding, repo, haircut, margin, collateral, CSA, CDS, TRS, ABS, MBS, coupon, YTM, duration, z-score, percentile, σ, sigma, regime, tie-out, backfill, dry-run

## Voice markers. Keep
- A specific odd detail, a first-person aside, a dated reference, a deliberate fragment
- Domain shorthand the reader shares (본포북, 팀전체, 누계, 대직자)
- Honorific level of the original

## Never add
- New facts, names, numbers, sources, claims. If a sentence needs a detail you lack, leave it or ask.
- New hedges, new "~해야 한다", new "~하는 이유다", new triads, new dashes.
